Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy explains how Quantum Mirage (“we”) collects, uses and protects personal data when you visit this site or play our games. The principle is simple: collect what we need, and say plainly what it is for.

1. Scope

This policy covers quantummiragelimited.com and its subpages, together with the games and services we publish and operate. It does not cover third-party sites or services we do not control.

2. What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service, namely:

  • Information you give us: the email address, display name and message you send when you get in touch.
  • Information generated automatically: page visits, browser language and device type.
  • In-game information: device model, OS version, crash logs and match statistics.

3. How we use it

We use this data to answer enquiries, fix defects, tune difficulty and matchmaking, and meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for cross-site behavioural advertising.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This site sets only essential cookies, used to remember your language and theme preferences. We do not set cross-site tracking cookies; where traffic measurement is needed, it relies on aggregate data with no personal identifiers.

5. Third-party services

Payments, cloud saves and crash reporting are handled by contracted providers. We share data only to the extent needed, and require protection no weaker than this policy.

6. Retention and security

Account data is kept while your account exists, and service logs for 90 days, unless the law requires otherwise. Traffic is encrypted with TLS, stored data is encrypted at rest, and access is granted on a least-privilege basis.

7. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn we have, we delete it once a guardian's identity is verified.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Write to privacy@quantummiragelimited.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will post a prominent notice on this site and update the date above. Questions about this policy: privacy@quantummiragelimited.com.
